# Student feedback instructions — PNS Research Proposal Report

Version 1.0 | 19 September 2026

Use this file together with:
- `grading_criteria.md`
- `GRADING_METHOD_Independent_Subcriteria.md`
- the frozen calibrated Stage 1 matrix / grade sheet
- the student's complete submission package

The feedback format is adapted from the current RDM version 2 feedback specification, but the content must be specific to the Psychobiology & Neuroscience Research Proposal Report.

## Authoritative calculation

Use the converted main-criterion percentages from the verified PNS grade sheet and the final processed whole-number overall grade.

Current PNS July 2026 calibration:
- target pre-penalty cohort mean M = 67
- target population SD S = 10
- criterion weights: 20 / 20 / 20 / 10 / 10 / 10 / 10
- one common cohort-derived affine conversion
- whole-number criterion display
- final whole-number overall allocated using mean-preserving cohort rounding
- word-count penalty handled separately after academic grading

Do not independently alter the narrative or criterion percentages to make them appear more intuitive. If the evidence contradicts an underlying Stage 1 competence code, amend the source matrix and recalculate the complete cohort before changing feedback.

## Required student-facing format

Aim for approximately **700–900 words total**, including headings and bullets.

1. Exact participant ID at the top.
2. **Strengths**: exactly three concise, specific bullets.
3. **Areas for Improvement**: exactly three concise, actionable bullets.
4. One heading per main criterion, in this order:
   - Knowledge and Understanding of the Topic / Issues
   - Research Methodology and Appropriate Implementation
   - Ethical Considerations
   - Criticality
   - Academic Integrity
   - Use of Relevant Sources
   - Structure and Presentation
5. Put the whole-number converted criterion percentage in each heading.
6. Write one connected narrative paragraph beneath each criterion heading.
7. End with **Overall grade — [H_i]%** and an 80–120 word summary of achievement and next steps.

Do not expose the 35 subcriterion names, internal codes, 0 / 0.5 / 1 scores, checklists, or internal calculation tables in student-facing feedback.

## Numerical explanation

Where a numerical explanation is useful, use:

> The criterion grades use the agreed cohort calibration. The overall combines their unrounded values using the University weights, followed by whole-number rounding that preserves the target class mean. Any applicable word-count penalty is then applied separately.

Do not describe the target mean or SD as a University requirement. They are assignment-calibration choices.

## Word-count penalty

The 2,000-word assignment permits up to 10% over the stated limit before penalty. A submission above **2,200 words** receives the prescribed **10 grade-point deduction** after the academically derived mark has been calculated.

Where this applies, display clearly:

**Academic grade before word-count penalty — [X]%**  
**Word-count penalty — 10 grade points**  
**Final grade — [Y]%**

Do not lower Structure and Presentation, or any other criterion, simply because the word-count threshold has been exceeded. The penalty must not be applied twice.

Use the student's declared assignment word count where that is the formal count supplied with the submission. Flag any uncertain count for manual checking rather than inventing one.

## Narrative quality and evidence

Address the student as **“you”**, use British English, and avoid contractions.

Feedback must be specific to the student's own proposal. Explain:
- what the student did;
- why it matters scientifically or methodologically;
- what needs strengthening;
- how the student could improve it.

Use concrete examples from the student's research question, theory, biological or neuropsychological construct, measures, design, statistical plan, ethics, limitations, source use, and presentation.

Praise should be evidence-based and proportionate. Occasional concise reinforcement such as “Good work here” or “You have done this well” is appropriate where supported.

Internally consider every underlying subcriterion, but combine related observations into one coherent paragraph per main criterion. Explain any material omission or weakness that substantially lowers a criterion mark.

## PNS-specific emphasis

In this assignment, feedback should pay particular attention to:
- whether the proposed study has a genuine psychobiological, cognitive-neuroscience or neuropsychological component rather than merely biological terminology;
- the mechanistic chain linking biological/physiological factors to psychological or behavioural outcomes;
- whether the literature identifies a bounded gap or justified extension;
- construct–measure alignment and the distinction between direct and indirect physiological indices;
- design–question and hypothesis–analysis alignment;
- causal claims versus correlational or observational evidence;
- statistical assumptions, interactions/moderation and power where relevant;
- feasibility for an online MSc project, including realistic access to equipment, assays, recruitment and technical expertise;
- study-specific ethics rather than generic boilerplate;
- sensitive populations, health interpretation, biological samples, physiological recording and neuroethical issues where relevant;
- evaluated limitations rather than lists;
- appropriate use of current, primary and methodological sources.

A technically sophisticated study should not receive extra credit merely because it is complicated. A simpler design can demonstrate full competence if it is well justified, feasible and well aligned.

## Academic integrity and source cautions

Do not infer plagiarism, unauthorised AI use, fabricated citations or other misconduct from writing style.

Do not invent citation or APA errors, source-verification findings, formatting defects or methodological permissions that have not been established from the submission.

If original formatting is unavailable or uncertain, state that limitation rather than penalising it as demonstrated fact.

## Final checks

Before finalising each feedback document:
- verify the exact participant ID;
- verify all seven criterion percentages against the grade sheet;
- verify the final pre-penalty and post-penalty grade where relevant;
- confirm exactly three Strengths bullets and three Areas for Improvement bullets;
- confirm one connected paragraph beneath each of the seven criterion headings;
- confirm the overall summary is 80–120 words;
- confirm total length is approximately 700–900 words;
- keep internal subcriterion labels and scores hidden;
- ground every substantive evaluative point in the student's submission;
- retain manual-review flags where appropriate.

Drafting and repository storage do not authorise sending or publishing feedback to students.
