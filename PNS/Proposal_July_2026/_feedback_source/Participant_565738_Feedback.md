## Strengths

- You treat HRV dynamically across baseline, reappraisal and recovery rather than assuming a single resting value captures regulatory functioning.

- Your primary regression isolates recovery-period lnRMSSD while controlling resting and reappraisal-period HRV and respiration, closely matching the research question.

- You critically distinguish physiological recovery from causal emotion-regulation ability and recognise demand characteristics, habituation and alternative strategy use.

## Areas for Improvement

- Reassess whether recruiting approximately 190–200 laboratory participants is realistic for the MSc timetable and available ECG resources.

- Address the fixed ordering of the reappraise block more explicitly, because always placing reappraisal last can confound regulation with time, fatigue and habituation.

- Prespecify the exact treatment of unusable ECG segments, respiratory covariation and participants who do not follow the reappraisal instruction.
