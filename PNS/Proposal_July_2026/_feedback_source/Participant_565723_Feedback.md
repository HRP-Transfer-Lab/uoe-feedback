## Ethical Considerations — 76%

Your ethical treatment of public EEG data is strong. For a complete proposal, state where the dataset and derived files will be stored, who will have access, what will be retained, and how any locally generated participant-level files will be handled. This is especially relevant because openly shared data can still be sensitive even when direct identifiers have been removed.

Good work here. You could go one step further by specifying how an unsupported H2 result would constrain interpretation of H3 and whether the reliability comparison should be viewed as confirmatory or primarily methodological given the available trial counts.
