## Ethical Considerations — 68%

You correctly recognise that the proposed project uses an openly available, de-identified secondary dataset and that no new participant consent is required for the new analysis if the original consent and data-sharing conditions permit reuse. You identify the original ethics approval and signed consent and note the minimal additional risk. State the dataset licence or terms of reuse, confirm that the planned analysis is compatible with the original consent, explain where the downloaded and derived files will be stored, who will have access and how long they will be retained. Secondary-data research still requires active ethical reasoning about governance and responsible reuse rather than only reduced participant risk.
