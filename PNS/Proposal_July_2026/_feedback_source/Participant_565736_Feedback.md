## Research Methodology and Appropriate Implementation — 63%

The design is well aligned. Threat-specific SCR is operationalised as threat minus neutral event-related amplitude, attentional bias is defined as incongruent minus congruent reaction time, and trait anxiety remains continuous. The main weakness is that the sample size is still provisional: “approximately 80” should be replaced by a completed interaction-focused power calculation. Practical feasibility also needs to be demonstrated because calibrated EDA equipment, controlled room conditions and around 80 individual recording sessions create a substantial workload in an online MSc context.

## Ethical Considerations — 76%

The ethics plan is strong and study-specific. You address mild discomfort from threatening faces, anxiety questions, the non-invasive nature of SCR, consent, withdrawal, pseudonymisation, data retention, distress procedures and the fact that the sensors do not read thoughts or provide a diagnosis. One issue should be made more explicit: the debrief states that the precise hypotheses were not fully explained before participation to avoid bias. That is a form of partial disclosure and should be justified prospectively in the ethics section, with clear explanation of why it is necessary, why risk remains minimal and how the debrief restores informed understanding after participation.

That would connect your methodological critique directly to the chosen outcome measure.
